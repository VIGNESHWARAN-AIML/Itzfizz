01 / KINEMATICS

Precision Scroll Interpolation

Every scroll increment maps linearly to composite transform matrices. Rather than forcing re-layouts with layout-triggering properties, the vehicle coordinates calculate ahead of render frames on isolated GPU layers with zero frame drops.

02 / AERODYNAMICS

Optimized ground-effect venturi channels and active rear wing vectoring reduce turbulence at high scroll speeds.

03 / TELEMETRY

Sub-Millisecond Response

Dual electric synchronous permanent magnet motors delivering instantaneous torque response without driveline lag.

Respect for User Motion Preferences
